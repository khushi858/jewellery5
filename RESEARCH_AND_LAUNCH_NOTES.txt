Design and content principles translated into AURELLE:
- Campaign-led first impression with one clear hero story and restrained copy.
- Editorial serif typography paired with a clean sans-serif interface.
- Warm neutral palette, a controlled gold accent, square/quiet calls to action and generous whitespace.
- Product discovery through collection categories, a dedicated collection finder, product badges, wishlist affordances, and catalogue filtering/sorting.
- On-body imagery for rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets, plus close-up campaign footage.
- Video playback controls and muted autoplay; video footage is external stock placeholder material.
- Gift discovery, personalisation/engraving enquiry concept, care information, brand journal, service promises and community-style image grid.
- Product detail page includes sizing, materials and delivery/returns content placeholders.
- Mobile responsive layouts and reduced visual motion compared with a typical effects-heavy template.

Important launch requirements:
- This is a front-end concept, not a production-connected store.
- Product names, prices, metal/stone descriptions, reviews, stock and policies are sample content and must be verified/replaced.
- Campaign images are generated concept visuals. Replace with approved AURELLE photography/product images.
- Pexels videos are external stock placeholders. Confirm the final video URLs/licensing and replace with approved brand footage before launch. If a source does not load in the target browser, replace it with a verified MP4 or hosted embed.
- Cart, payment, customer accounts, inventory, search backend, personalisation workflow, newsletter delivery and order management are not connected.
- Add real brand contact details, social profile URLs, shipping/returns terms, privacy and terms pages before launch.
- Accessibility review, performance optimisation, SEO metadata, analytics, consent management and cross-browser QA remain required.
