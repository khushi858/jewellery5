AURELLE — LUXURY JEWELLERY WEBSITE, ENHANCED EDITION

Open index.html in a modern browser.

Pages:
- index.html: dark cinematic campaign hero, timed slides, expanded 12-product featured edit, model-worn category imagery, campaign mosaic, multiple video demonstrations, editorial statements, reviews and newsletter.
- collections.html: rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets plus extended product edit.
- product.html: product detail, imagery, size selector, details and related products.
- story.html: brand story, jewellery care and FAQs.

Original campaign visuals:
- Seven locally included AI-generated images in assets/ (six square product/model campaign shots and one wide hero campaign image).
- Visuals focus on jewellery worn on fingers/hands, neck/collarbone, ears and wrists, plus macro product details.

Motion:
- 6.2-second hero carousel, manual controls, pause on hover and animated text entrances.
- Loading intro, sticky compact header, scroll progress.
- Scroll reveal, left/right transitions and staggered product cards.
- Slow image zoom, parallax drift and product tilt.
- Multiple muted looping jewellery videos play while visible.
- Animated marquee and responsive campaign mosaic.
